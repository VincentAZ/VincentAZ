## Runs on your own computer

- **[Pulse](https://github.com/VincentAZ/pulse-monitor)**<br>
  Mission Center and nvtop in one window. A native GTK 4 system and GPU monitor for Linux: CPU, memory, disks, network, every GPU and every process touching a GPU, read straight from the kernel. Mounts drives from the disk page, no daemons, nothing leaves your machine. MIT.
- **[Desktop Buddy](https://github.com/VincentAZ/Desktop-Buddy)**<br>
  Tell your Linux desktop what you want done. A native, local-first AI assistant for GNOME that works with files, apps, browsers, terminals, images and your screen—with your choice of cloud or local model.
- **[Digital Human Studio](https://github.com/VincentAZ/DigitalHumanStudio)**<br>
  Give your character a direction. Build a performance. A local Linux studio for directing virtual humans with AI, composing reusable motion skills, and generating movement from text. NVIDIA Isaac Sim, skinned characters, and your choice of local or cloud assistant.
- **[prompt-maker](https://github.com/VincentAZ/prompt-maker)**<br>
  Say what you want to see, in plain words, and get pictures and videos made on your own computer. It writes the prompt each model likes, keeps the same person from shot to shot and gives them a voice. Nothing leaves your machine unless you opt in, and it's free.
- **[NewsletterCreator](https://github.com/VincentAZ/NewsletterCreator)** · [latest release](https://github.com/VincentAZ/NewsletterCreator/releases/latest)<br>
  Point it at your sources and get a newsletter back. AI on your own computer sorts the stories, drafts the edition and makes the pictures; hand the draft to Mailchimp or export HTML. Nothing leaves your machine unless you opt in. Free, no account.
- **[clipdeck](https://github.com/VincentAZ/clipdeck)**<br>
  Local video repair, editing, and conversion for GNOME, with verified outputs and batch processing.
- **[shreder](https://github.com/VincentAZ/shreder)**<br>
  Linux desktop app for file overwriting, free-space wiping, and scheduled privacy cleanup. Built with Python, GTK 4, and libadwaita.

## Web apps

- **[StackWorks](https://github.com/VincentAZ/StackWorks)** · [try it](https://www.stackworksai.tech/)<br>
  How software works, as a 3D machine you can walk around, take apart and run. Seventeen stations connected by glass pipes, eight animated scenarios, a decision lab, and a blueprint builder that writes the build brief to hand your AI coding assistant. Built for nontechnical founders. No backend, no tracking. MIT.
- **[Crave](https://github.com/VincentAZ/crave-restaurant-finder)** · [try it](https://crave-restaurant-finder.vercel.app)<br>
  Rate a few dishes, enter your ZIP code, and find nearby restaurants on a map. Search by distance or estimated drive time, edit your food choices, and check source-backed local ownership badges. No visitor account or API key required.
- **[familywatchlist](https://github.com/VincentAZ/familywatchlist)** · [try it](https://www.familywatchlist.tv)<br>
  Shared TV & movie watchlist for households — Next.js, Supabase, Stripe subscriptions, metered AI credits, multi-provider AI assistant. MIT.
